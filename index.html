Hi, I'm Will! I'm a behavioral ecologist fascinated by the feedback between animal behavior and parasitism. I’m currently a 5th-year Ph.D. student with Vanessa Ezenwa in the Ecology and Evolutionary Biology Department at Yale. My work focuses on the role that behavior - particularly social interactions and movement ecology - play in shaping disease outcomes, and how parasites in turn shape their host's behavior. I'm interested in better understanding how these feedbacks scale-up to shape epidemics, social dynamics, and host ecology.

This site is under active development - Please let me know if you find any errors!

Updates

  • [Spring 2026]    Work with Timm Wild, Vanessa Ezenwa , and colleagues at MPI-AB was accepted at Movement Ecology. Stay tuned for article. This work validates a WiFi proximity logging platform and shows how multi-sensor platforms can improve RSSI-based inferences. For instance, weather data and accelerometer data collected simultaneously with RSSI were able to significantly improve distance predictions based on RSSI. We also directly compare GPS- and RSSI-based proximity inferences, with some important considerations for fine-scale spatial inferences. There is a lot more in the paper, and this initial effort sets up some exciting future work including work on disease-induced changes in buffalo social interactions - stay tuned :)
